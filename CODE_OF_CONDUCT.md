# Code of Conduct

Sentinel Swarm is an engineering project and its community should be a place where people can challenge ideas without attacking people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and technically constructive.
- Assume good faith while still asking for evidence.
- Welcome contributors regardless of background or experience level.
- Keep security discussions responsible and avoid publishing active exploit details when private reporting is appropriate.
- Critique code, claims, tests, and architecture — not the person behind them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and targeted abuse are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive. Serious security concerns should be reported through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
